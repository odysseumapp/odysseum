## At a glance

- **Age:** 61
- **Occupation:** Runs the last ferry to Bellwether
- **Home:** The harbor office, though he says he only sleeps there
- **Habit:** Answers every question with a slightly different question

## What he knows

Tomas ferried Mara's father to the island the day he disappeared, and he has never told anyone what was said on that crossing. He recognizes Mara's name before she gives it.

## How he sounds

Short sentences. Weather first, then people. He calls the island "her" and never explains why.
