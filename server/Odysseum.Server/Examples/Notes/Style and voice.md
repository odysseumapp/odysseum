## Sentences

Short, plain, concrete. One image per paragraph. Dialogue without tags wherever the speaker is obvious.

## The island

Nobody explains it. Nobody uses the word *magic*. The islanders talk about it the way people talk about tides.

## Objects that do the work

The kettle. The pencil. The little map. The silver frame. The loose plank on the jetty.

## Things to avoid

Explaining the mechanism. Adjectives for the sea. Letting Tomas answer a question.
