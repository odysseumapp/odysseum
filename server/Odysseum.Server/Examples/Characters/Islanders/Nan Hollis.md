## At a glance

- **Age:** 66
- **Occupation:** Shopkeeper, postmistress, keeper of the harbor noticeboard
- **Home:** The rooms above the shop
- **Habit:** Writes down everything anyone tells her, in pencil, in a ledger nobody is allowed to read

## What she keeps

The ledger is, in effect, a second map of the island: a list of places, and beside each one the name of the last person who mentioned it. When a name is crossed out, the place is on its way.

## How she sounds

Brisk, kind, relentlessly specific. She remembers what you bought twelve years ago and tells you.
