## At a glance

- **Age:** 44 when he disappeared
- **Occupation:** Coastal surveyor for the county, cartographer by temperament
- **Home:** The white house above the harbor, then nowhere anyone can point to
- **Habit:** Sharpens pencils with a knife and keeps the shavings in his coat pocket

## What is known

He crossed to the mainland on *The Patience* on a Tuesday and came back on the same boat that evening. He was seen walking up the hill road. He was not seen again.

## What he left

An atlas with one blank page. A second map, palm-sized, in his daughter’s handwriting, that changes when nobody is looking. A rule about promises to strangers.

## What he sounds like in memory

Patient, dry, quick to laugh and then quick to stop. He answers a child’s questions as if they were the important ones.
