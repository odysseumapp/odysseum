## Start

A cartographer who trusts the ruler. Faces the door. Has not been on a boat in twelve years.

## Middle

Every measurement she takes on Bellwether is wrong by the morning. She keeps taking them anyway, which is either courage or the opposite.

## End

She draws the island and leaves the northern shore blank, not because she does not know it, but because she has finally understood what her father meant about the sea.
