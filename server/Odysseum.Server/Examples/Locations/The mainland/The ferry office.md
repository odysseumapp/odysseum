## The office

A wooden hut with a hatch, a bench, and a timetable under cracked glass. The timetable is accurate for every destination except one.

Tomas does not work in the office. He sits outside it on an overturned crate, mending nets, and answers the questions the timetable will not.

## The jetty

At the far end, a small blue boat rises and falls against its ropes. Under three coats of paint, a name.

## Rule

The ferry to Bellwether runs when someone needs it to. It is not listed because nobody who needs it would believe the timetable anyway.
