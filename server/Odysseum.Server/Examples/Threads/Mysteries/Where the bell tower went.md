## The question

How does a stone tower cross a mile of island without disturbing a sheep?

## What each islander says

- **Quill:** He rang it across. Sixty-one rings. Or sixty.
- **Nan:** Somebody crossed the eastern spit out of the ledger.
- **Elin:** It is where Mara remembers it. Ask her where that is.

## Resolution

It goes back east the morning after Mara remembers climbing it, and nobody, including Mara, sees it happen.
