## The sea does not move

Coastlines shift. The harbor stays where the water lets it. The bell tower crosses the island, but the water it stands in is the same water.

## What that means

The sea is the only honest map. Aldous knew it: *never pretend the blank is the sea.* Blank is what you do not know. The sea is what you do.

## For the ending

Mara leaves the northern shore blank. The sea around it she draws exactly.
