## The island

A harbor village, a lighthouse, a narrow road through the hills. The sort of place people leave and then spend their lives describing to strangers.

Bellwether is not on any current chart.

## Places that matter

- **The harbor:** The only reliable point of arrival.
- **The lighthouse:** Three windows and a crooked door. Elin has kept the light for forty years.
- **The white house:** Mara’s childhood home, above the village.
- **The bell tower:** No longer where anyone remembers it.

## Rules to keep in mind

The island changes slowly when people forget. It changes quickly when someone remembers something they have tried very hard to lose.

The sea itself does not change. It is the one thing that keeps an honest record.
