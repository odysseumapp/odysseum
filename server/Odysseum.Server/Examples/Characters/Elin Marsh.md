## At a glance

- **Age:** 70
- **Occupation:** Keeper of the Bellwether light, retired in name only
- **Home:** The white house above the harbor
- **Habit:** Feeds people before she tells them anything

## What she is hiding

Elin drew the first map of the island's changing coastline, decades before Mara's father did. She knows the places that vanish are the ones nobody visits, and she has been visiting them, alone, for years.

## How she sounds

Warm, practical, unhurried. She never lies, but she chooses which truths arrive first.
