## At a glance

- **Age:** Somewhere past eighty
- **Occupation:** Rings the bell. Nobody appointed him.
- **Home:** A single room at the foot of the tower, wherever the tower happens to be
- **Habit:** Counts the rings out loud, then disagrees with the count

## What he does

When a place on the island shifts, the bell rings once. Quill insists he rings it. Elin says the bell rings and Quill runs to stand under it. Both of them are probably right.

## How he sounds

Cheerful, unbothered, a little deaf. He calls Mara “the surveyor’s girl” and cannot be persuaded she has grown up.
