## At a glance

- **Age:** Died at 58, three years before the letter
- **Occupation:** Schoolteacher on the island, then on the mainland
- **Home:** A rented house in the city with no maps on the walls
- **Habit:** Faced with anything impossible, she put the kettle on

## What she knew

Ines had a feeling the morning Aldous left, and she took Mara up to the lighthouse to wait it out with Elin. She never told Mara she had been there. She let her forget.

## Why she left

Not grief. The island had started to change too quickly around a house where two people were remembering the same man in different ways.

## How she sounds

Brisk, affectionate, exact. Three minutes is long enough for anything.
