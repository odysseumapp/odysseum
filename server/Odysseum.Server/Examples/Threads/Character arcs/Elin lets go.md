## Start

Warm, practical, unhurried. Feeds people before she tells them anything. Visits the places nobody else remembers, alone, so they will not go.

## Middle

Mara’s arrival makes some of those places move on their own. Elin realises that her remembering has been holding the island still, and holding Mara’s father in it.

## End

She lets the fourth window stay bricked up. She lets the northern shore go. She says nothing when Mara leaves it blank, which is the kindest thing she has ever said.
