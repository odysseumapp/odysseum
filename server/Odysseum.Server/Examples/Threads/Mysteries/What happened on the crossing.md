## The question

What did Aldous say to Tomas on *The Patience*, and why has Tomas answered every question since with a slightly different question?

## Clues in order

1. Tomas recognises Mara’s name before she gives it.
2. He calls the island “her”.
3. He tells Mara to put the map away until she has decided what she is looking for.
4. He waits at the jetty with the engine running, every time, as if someone else might still come down the hill.

## The answer (do not write down yet)

Aldous asked him to keep bringing the boat.
