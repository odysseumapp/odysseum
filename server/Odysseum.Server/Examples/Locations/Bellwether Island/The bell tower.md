## The tower

Square, grey, older than the village. One bell, no clock. Sixty-one steps, which Mara climbed on a dare at ten and counted on the way down.

## Where it is

Ask three islanders and get three answers, all of them sincere. It was on the eastern spit when Mara was a child. From the deck of *The Patience* it stands to starboard, which means west, which means it has moved a mile across the island without disturbing a single sheep.

## The bell

Rings once when a place shifts. Father Quill claims responsibility. The bell does not seem to need him.
