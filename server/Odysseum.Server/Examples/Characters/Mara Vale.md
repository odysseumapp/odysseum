## At a glance

- **Age:** 28
- **Occupation:** Cartographer for a city planning office
- **Home:** A small flat above a bakery
- **Habit:** Makes tea when she needs time to think

## What she wants

An explanation for her father’s disappearance. A world that stays where she puts it.

## What she needs

To understand that remembering is not the same as knowing, and that going home does not mean becoming the person she was.

## Small, useful details

She keeps pencils until they are too short to hold. She always sits facing the door. She has not been on a boat in twelve years.

She can draw the shape of Bellwether with her eyes closed.
