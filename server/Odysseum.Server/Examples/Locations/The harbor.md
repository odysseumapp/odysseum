## The place

A stone jetty, a curve of low houses, a shop with a noticeboard, and a slipway where boats go to be forgotten about. The harbor faces north-west into the weather, which everyone agrees was a mistake made by someone else.

## What to notice

- The jetty has one loose plank near the end. It has been loose for thirty years.
- The noticeboard outside Nan’s shop is the island’s memory in public.
- *The Patience* is tied up at the end of the jetty, or is not, depending on whether Tomas expects anyone.

## Rule

Nobody has ever arrived on Bellwether anywhere else. Nobody has tried.
