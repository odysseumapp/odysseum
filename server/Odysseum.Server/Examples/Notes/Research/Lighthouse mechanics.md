## Clockwork rotation

Older lights turn the lens on a weight-driven clockwork: a heavy weight descends a tube through the tower and is wound back up by hand, every few hours through the night. The keeper hears the escapement tick and the lens bearings turn.

## The sound

A slow, rolling grind with a click at the top of each turn. It never stops while the light is lit. From the kitchen it is felt as much as heard.

## Elin’s light

Electrified in the seventies but still on the original bearings. She winds nothing now and climbs anyway.
