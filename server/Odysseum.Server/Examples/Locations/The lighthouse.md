## The building

A squat white tower on the crooked shoulder of the island, with a keeper’s house grown onto its side like a barnacle. Three windows face the sea. The door has hung slightly wrong since a storm in Elin’s first year and she has never let anyone fix it.

There used to be a fourth window. It was bricked up the winter after the photograph was taken. Nobody mentions it.

## Inside

Beeswax, onions, boots in a row. A kitchen full of afternoon light. A photograph in a silver frame on the windowsill that Elin keeps meaning to put away.

Above, the lamp machinery turns with a slow sound that has never learned to rest.

## Why it does not move

Elin remembers it every day, deliberately, the way other people say prayers.
