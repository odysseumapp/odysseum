## The flat

Two rooms above a bakery, so it is always slightly too warm and smells of bread at five in the morning. A drawing table by the window. A narrow kitchen with a kettle that clicks off louder than it needs to.

Upstairs, a pianist who has been learning the same piece for a year.

## The shelf

Work maps, rolled. Reference books. One atlas, older than the rest, that she has not opened in twelve years.

## The view

Buses, a taxi rank, rain that pretends not to notice anyone. The city is very good at staying where you put it.
