## Tidal windows

A shallow bay with a bar at its mouth can only be crossed by a keeled boat for a few hours either side of high water. Twice a day, later each day by about fifty minutes.

## Timetables

Printed timetables for tidal ferries list *typical* sailings and a note nobody reads. A ferry that runs “when someone needs it to” is, technically, within the note.

## For the book

Tomas never checks the tide. The tide is right when he sails. Mara notices this on the second crossing and does not mention it.
