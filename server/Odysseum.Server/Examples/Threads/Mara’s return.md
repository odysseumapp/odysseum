## The line

Letter → ferry → lighthouse → the white house → the northern shore → the ferry again.

## What it is about

Going home does not mean becoming the person you were. Mara has to arrive, and then she has to *arrive*, which takes the rest of the book.

## Beats to keep

- She almost does not open the letter.
- The timetable denies the island exists.
- Elin feeds her before telling her anything.
- She leaves the northern shore blank on purpose.
